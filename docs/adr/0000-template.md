# NNNN. Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed
- **Discussion:** link to the GitHub discussion

## Context

What forces are in play. What is true today that makes a decision necessary. Include
the constraints that rule out alternatives — those are the part a future reader cannot
reconstruct.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated in the active voice. Be specific enough that someone could
implement it from this paragraph alone.

## Alternatives considered

Each with why it was rejected. This is the most valuable section; a rejected
alternative with a clear reason saves the next person the whole investigation.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to live with. Include
new obligations — a decision that only adds capability without adding work is usually
missing something.

## Revisit when

The condition that would invalidate this decision. Without it, ADRs become permanent
by neglect rather than by judgement.
